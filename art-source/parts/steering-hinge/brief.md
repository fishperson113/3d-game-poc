# Steering hinge visual brief

Low-poly steering knuckle inspired by Fischertechnik and LEGO Technic front hubs. A visible 0.40 m arm bridges the chassis kingpin at local `[-0.20, 0, 0]` to the wheel axle at `[0.20, 0, 0]`. The kingpin is tall, bright and vertical; the outer axle stub is metallic and points along local +X. Variant A uses one solid arm, while variant B uses two fork cheeks. Both include a contrasting steering tab and diagonal brace so the mechanism remains readable when the wheel is mounted.

The steering axis is local +Y and passes through the `mount` socket. The wheel-spin axis is local +X through the `axle` socket. The origin is halfway between both sockets. Decorative geometry must not imply suspension that physics does not implement.

Provenance: internal procedural design brief, no external reference image or third-party art.
