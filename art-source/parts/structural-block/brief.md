# Structural block visual brief

Low-poly Besiege-like construction block, one metre part frame, Y-up and +Z forward. Variant A is a solid chamfered blue-grey block with four inset fasteners. Variant B is a visibly different open braced frame with two diagonal orange struts and a smaller central plate. The origin is the body center; visual geometry must remain a child of the authoritative part frame.

Provenance: internal procedural design brief, no external reference image or third-party art.
