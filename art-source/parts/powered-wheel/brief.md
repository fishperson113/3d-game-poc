# Powered wheel visual brief

Low-poly powered wheel centred on the axle hub. Variant A is a thick dark rubber cylinder with a cyan hub and ring. Variant B has a narrower tyre, a bright hub and six visible spokes. The axle is local +X; the part frame origin is the hub center. Visual changes must not change the socket or collider contract.

Provenance: internal procedural design brief, no external reference image or third-party art.
